/* Ammo Making: Spent Cases (add-on) - scrapping and resizing spent brass.

   EXPERIMENTAL, NOT YET SEEN IN GAME.

   THE BODY OF THIS FILE IS GENERATED by tests/write_recipes.lua from
   AC_Recycling.lua of the main mod (the source "spent") and from
   AC_SpentCases.RESIZE; this comment is kept. Do not edit the body.

   SCRAPPING

   One recipe for each size of case, exactly like the main mod's
   AmmoMaking_ScrapBrass<units> recipes (cold, on a surface, a hammer
   kept), with one difference: the recovery.

     unused components   20 units of brass in, 1 brass scrap out   a half
     spent cases         40 units of brass in, 1 brass scrap out   a quarter

   No XP. The scrap is vanilla Base.BrassScrap and is recast by the main
   mod's AmmoMaking_CastBrassIngotFromScrap.

   RESIZING

   AmmoMaking_ResizeSpentCase<calibre> <- the main mod's AmmoMaking_FormCase<calibre>
                                          (cold, on a surface, the calibre's die set
                                          and a hammer kept), two spent cases of the
                                          calibre in place of the case cups

   Two spent cases in, one usable case out: the other cracks and its brass is
   lost. No XP, the forming recipe's time, one Ammo Making level above it.
   The case is the main mod's own item; it is assembled like any other, and
   its casing quality is rolled lower than a newly formed case's
   (docs/SPENT_CASE_RESEARCH.md 8).

   The OnCreate functions are defined by the main mod when this add-on is
   active; it also attaches each recipe's Ammo Making level. */
