/* Ammo Making: Spent Cases (add-on) - the spent case of each calibre.

   EXPERIMENTAL, NOT YET SEEN IN GAME. This file is in the ADD-ON mod
   AmmoMakingSpentCases. Ticking the add-on is the feature switch
   "spentCases" (AC_Features.lua in the main mod); without it these items
   do not exist and nothing leaves them.

   THE BODY OF THIS FILE IS GENERATED by tests/write_recipes.lua
   (AC_SpentCases.buildItems in the main mod); this comment is kept. Do not
   edit the body.

   One item per calibre of the calibre model. A spent case is NOT a case:
   no recipe loads it as it is. It is scrapped, at a quarter of its brass,
   or resized at the calibre's die set, two spent cases into one usable
   case (both in AC_SpentCaseRecipes.txt, both for no XP).

   PLACEHOLDER_VISUAL: every field but the name is copied from the
   calibre's own unused case in the main mod's AC_Items.txt, so a spent
   case has the icon, the world model and the weight of the case it was.
   Those are vanilla placeholders themselves (docs/PLACEHOLDER_ASSETS.md).
   A spent case with a look of its own is an Icon and a WorldStaticModel
   here, through the generator. */
